Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how the Dilshan Tec WhatsApp assistant handles your information.

📥 What we collect

When you message our WhatsApp assistant, we receive your WhatsApp phone number and the messages you send.

⚙️ How we use it

Your messages are used only to generate replies. They are processed by Anthropic's Claude AI service to create the response, and delivered through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta).

⏳ How long we keep it

Recent messages are kept temporarily so the assistant can follow the conversation. They are deleted when you send "/reset" or when our server restarts. We do not keep a permanent record of your chats.

🤝 Sharing

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. We share it only with the services needed to run the assistant:

  • Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, to receive and send messages
  • Anthropic, to generate replies

✅ Your choices

You can stop using the assistant at any time, send "/reset" to clear your conversation, or contact us to ask about or delete your data.
